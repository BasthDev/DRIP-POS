
> A modern, multi-store Point of Sale and inventory management platform built for businesses that need more than a simple cashier.

DRIP POS is designed as a **scalable, multi-tenant POS platform** for coffee shops, restaurants, retail stores, and growing businesses.

It combines **POS, inventory, purchasing, recipes, HPP/COGS, multi-store management, batch tracking, FIFO/FEFO, and business analytics** into one system.

---

## ✨ What is DRIP POS?

DRIP POS is built around one simple idea:

> **Your business should be able to grow without outgrowing its POS.**

A single business owner can manage multiple stores from one account while keeping each store's sales, inventory, warehouse, employees, and operations properly separated.
